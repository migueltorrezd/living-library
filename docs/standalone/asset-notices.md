The application, reconstructed geometry, publisher artwork, quotations, public-domain texts and author-provided PDFs are different kinds of material. Inclusion in this demonstration does not assign the same license to all of them.

Publisher covers, book titles, marks, photographs and quoted praise remain attributable to their respective authors, publishers and other rights holders. Source URLs accompany each edition. No publisher endorsement is claimed. A matching ISBN does not guarantee an identical printing.

The historical texts referenced through Project Gutenberg are separate from the modern publisher editions displayed by the models. Preserve the supplied source and license notices. Territorial public-domain status can differ. The author's freely offered Naval PDF is fetched from its original source when requested; it is not bundled as a local book file.

The JavaScript PDF worker is an unmodified file from the pinned `pdfjs-dist` dependency. Retain its license header and the dependency's notices. The other third-party software dependencies retain their own licenses.

No blanket open-source or commercial-reuse license is asserted for all bundled visual content. Before distributing a collection under a license of your own, distinguish the code and model work you control from the third-party printed art and texts. Replace or separately clear artwork as required for that distribution.

Source pixels, approximated backs, edition discrepancies and reconstructed binding details are documented in the catalogue's source material. The package does not use AI enlargement to invent sharper lettering, and it does not remove watermarks or copyright marks from source artwork.
